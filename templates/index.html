About AURA Platform

AURA is trained locally on synthetic parameters matching typical public health infections and skin conditions. Since it works 100% offline, it represents a lightweight, portable clinical support system ideal for remote villages, military posts, and offline disaster response sectors.

Offline Architecture Summary

  • **Natural Language Engine**: Tokenized dictionary structures mapping multi-word symptom variations in 9 languages client-side.
  • **Classifier Model**: Binary symptom vectors are classified via locally serialised scikit-learn Random Forests.
  • **Image Feature Extractor**: RGB color indices, HSV saturation parameters, and image spatial gradient texture maps.
  • **Speech Synthesis**: Offline Web Speech Synthesizers executing localized voices.
⚠️ **Clinical Support Disclaimer Notice**: AURA is a diagnostic assistance simulator trained on synthetic data for Smart India Hackathon demonstrations and placements. It does not provide professional medical advice. Always seek professional care for clinical decisions.
