About AURA Platform

Autonomous & Offline AI-Driven Public Health Assistant for Disease Awareness & Clinical Decision Support

AURA Platform is a next-generation, community-centered public health engine developed to operate with 100% offline autonomy. It provides instant disease awareness, predictive symptom diagnostics, visual skin pathology scanning, clinical vitals evaluation, and emergency triage without requiring internet access or external cloud servers. It guarantees complete patient data privacy with zero data leakage.

PLATFORM MODULES

Core Clinical Capabilities

💬

Multilingual Symptom Checker

Interactive offline chatbot parsing physical symptoms across 34 infectious, chronic, and epidemic conditions in English, हिन्दी, and తెలుగు.

📷

Visual Skin Pathology Scanner

Edge computer-vision feature extractor evaluating RGB color distribution, HSV saturation gradients, and texture metrics for rash, eczema, and acne.

📈

Clinical Telemetry & Live ECG

Accurate Blood Pressure formulas (Mean Arterial Pressure, Pulse Pressure, AHA Stage Classification) accompanied by a live 60 FPS Canvas ECG visualizer.

⏰

Offline Medicine Reminder

Digital medication scheduler with browser-native sound alerts, dosage tracking, and encrypted client-side localStorage persistence.

🎮

Mindful Pause (4-7-8 Technique)

Guided diaphragmatic breathing cycle (4s inhale → 7s hold → 8s exhale) to reduce heart rate variability and relieve acute anxiety.

🚨

SOS Critical Warning Protocol

Autonomous safety monitor detecting critical vital thresholds (SpO2 < 94%, severe hypertensive crisis) with an emergency acoustic siren alert.
